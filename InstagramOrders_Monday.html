About the bakery

  1. Cupcakes are $40 a dozen and take 30 minutes in the oven. Cakes are $120 each and take 2 hours. Each order is for one product.
  2. The oven runs 8 hours a day. A day's orders can never need more oven hours than the day has. Each order is baked on its pickup day. Customers pick up at 5 PM.
  3. Everything needs 2 days' notice: orders, changes, cancellations and booking a helper. A message sent Monday can be for Wednesday or later.
  4. The baker says yes or no to every new order. The app should show which orders can be taken without breaking these rules.
  5. A helper costs $100 and adds 4 oven hours to one day.
  6. Cake orders usually come a week ahead, while most cupcake orders come in the last 3 days. Saturday is the busiest day.
